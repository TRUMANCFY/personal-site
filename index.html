Bio

I work on Information Retrieval (IR), Large Language Models (LLMs), and agentic RL. My research studies retrieval from three perspectives: (1) Embeddings: how do embeddings function in retrieval systems? (GeoHard) (2) Granularity: how should retrieval granularity be determined? (MixGR) (3) Supervision: how can strong retrievers be trained cheaply in specialized domains such as science and code? (Revela, ElicitR, CoQuIR)

I am a final-year Ph.D. student at TU Darmstadt, Germany, co-supervised by Heinz Koeppl and Iryna Gurevych, and I expect to graduate by the end of 2026. In summer 2026 I was a Research Intern at Microsoft (Office of Applied Research, Redmond), working on long-horizon agentic RL for LLM post-training. Before my Ph.D., I obtained my M.Sc. in Computer Science at École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), supervised by Boi Faltings.
